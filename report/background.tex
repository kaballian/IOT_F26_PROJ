\subsection{MQTT communication protocol}
Message queuing telemetry transport \textit{(MQTT)} is a lightweight communication protocol,
designed for transmissions between IoT systems. It implements a subscribe/publish model, in which
devices communicate through a broker, instead of directly to one another. A device publish
a specific topic, while other devices can subscribe to that particular topic. This enables
increased scalability between distributed systems. This is a particular model is well suited
for embedded solutions with low bandwidth requirements and minimal overhead, due to ie. power
contrains or simple processor overhead.\\ 
In IoT environments MQTT topocs are commonly organized into hierarchies in order to separate 
different types of data, whether it be measurements or other types of information. 

\subsection{Time-Series Databases and InfluxDB}
Environmental monitoring systems continuously generates timestamped data. This data must be 
stored to create a log of both current and post behavior. Conventional relational databases
are not optimized for this, due to ingestion speed at scale. For this task, time-series databases
are designed to handle timestamped data efficiently.\\
InfluxDB is an open-source time-series database commonly used in IoT.

\subsection{Embedded systems and microcontrollers}
Embedded systems are smaller specialized computing systems designed to perform
smaller tasks within larger electronic systems. The operation is deterministic, which
results in predictible behavior and low-power consumption. They main purpose is to interface
to hardware, to bridge the communication to sensors, format data and pass it along.\\
microcontrollers also feature deditcated hardware, which can be repurposed to control 
external hardware such as fans and do voltagem measurements.
